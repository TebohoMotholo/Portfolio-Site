About Me

Who I Am

I’m Teboho Motholo, an IT professional who started in technical support and now specializes in IT systems administration and network administration. I’m an IT Support Technician at Redial BPO in Johannesburg, and I run my own technology company, Mozy Information Technology, from Phuthaditjhaba. I like solving problems and I like building things that make people’s work easier.

Professional Background

I’m currently an IT Support Technician at Redial BPO, where I specialize in systems and network administration, supporting a multi-campaign contact centre environment across South Africa, the US and Mexico. My work covers Microsoft Intune, Microsoft 365 and Entra ID, endpoint security with Microsoft Defender, and OS imaging. I built a production FOG imaging server for the floor PCs, and I’ve been part of the move from SCCM to Intune, the Windows Autopilot rollout and the current Windows Autopatch pilot. Before Redial I spent about two years at Finbond Mutual Bank supporting 170+ branches nationally, and before that I worked at Makro Bloemfontein and Moteka Secondary School. In total that is around four and a half years of enterprise IT support.

My Personal Story

My journey began in a small school where I helped teachers with basic tech issues. That early exposure to solving real problems with simple tools lit a spark in me. Since then, I’ve completed a BSc in Information Technology at North-West University, earned my Microsoft 365 Fundamentals (MS-900) certification, and kept learning. I build a Proxmox home lab to practise networking, Active Directory and security, and I take on training in data analytics, software development, and cloud and Kubernetes. I believe the best way to stay relevant in tech is to stay hungry for knowledge. I want to be a multi-skilled professional who can work across support, infrastructure and development, and that’s the direction I’m heading.

Looking Ahead

Looking ahead, I’m building on my support and endpoint experience in these areas:

Cloud & Infrastructure: deepening my skills in Azure, Intune, networking and virtualization, and working towards CompTIA A+, Network+ and CCNA.

Software & Web Development: building practical web applications for small businesses and communities through Mozy IT, and growing my full-stack skills.

Data & Analytics: using data analysis and dashboards to help teams make better decisions.
