Case study

Endpoint migration lab

A Proxmox lab for practising an SCCM to Intune migration end to end.

  • In progress
  • Proxmox VE
  • pfSense
  • Active Directory
  • Intune
  • SCCM

Goal

Practise a full SCCM to Intune migration somewhere safe: Group Policy, apps, reporting, line-of-business authentication and the Intune Suite features. It builds on the migration I took part in at Redial BPO, where I worked alongside the engineer who led it.

The lab

Hover, tap or tab to a block for details.

What the lab covers

  • Moving Group Policy settings to Intune policies.
  • Packaging and deploying apps through Intune.
  • Reporting on devices and apps.
  • Authentication for line-of-business apps.
  • Intune Suite features such as Endpoint Privilege Management.

Where it is now

The first phase is under way: a Windows 11 template, extra client machines, and an inventory of the GPOs and apps that need to move. A second domain controller is planned as an Active Directory backup. The lab runs on a PC with 32 GB of RAM, with Proxmox installed on its own NVMe SSD.
